    The Heritage Glossary

    Textile History dates back to the beginning of human civilization. Hartwick Atelier specializes in hand spun and handwoven fabrics, natural and ayurvedic dyes, and exquisite heritage handwork. These artisanal techniques rely on rare knowledge and the natural cycles of the sun, wind and rain.

    Our Story & Sustainability

    Cloth With A Living Pulse

    Hartwick was founded to preserve the rarest handspun, handwoven, and naturally dyed textile lineages of India—translating centuries-old master craft into quiet, architectural silhouettes designed to be worn and cherished for decades.

    Every metre of cloth begins in village ateliers where cotton and wild silk are spun without electricity on the Charkha wheel, steeped in living vats of wild indigo, madder root, pomegranate rind, and turmeric, and woven on pit looms.

    Each garment is cut and French-seamed by a single master tailor in small numbered editions—honouring the human hand at every stage from Fibre to Garment.
